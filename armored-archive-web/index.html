Calculation, sources and limits

Protection is evaluated for the selected hit zone and ammunition mechanism. Representative steel/ERA paths use layer-specific properties; sourced frontal RHAe examples use the 0° shell table directly. Unknown composite/NERA geometry produces an unresolved result. The ±5% shell band is datamine dispersion, not hit probability. Estimated armour bands are separate modelling uncertainty. Ricochet, overmatch, projectile shatter, internal damage and blast are not fully simulated. A favourable result applies only to the selected zone and fit.

Optional full-calibre formula: P = v^1.43 × m^0.71 ÷ (1900^1.43 × (d/100)^1.07) × 100 × explosive-fill factor × cap factor. v in m/s, m in kg, d in mm. APCBC cap factor = 1; other supported AP types = 0.9. Range/angle retention comes from published tables. APDS/APFSDS and chemical rounds keep their own Wiki values.
